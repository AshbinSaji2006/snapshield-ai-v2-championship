# Project summary

SnapShield AI is a private, local cyber-defense assistant for suspicious messages, links, screenshots, QR codes, and sensitive-data exposure. A real UCI-trained ONNX classifier works alongside URL, social-engineering, Unicode, brand-impersonation, OCR, QR, and PII engines. SnapShield produces a transparent risk score, exact evidence, redaction, and practical next steps without contacting submitted destinations or cloud AI. Its QDQ/QNN deployment boundary and validation harness are implemented; target Snapdragon NPU validation remains explicitly pending.
