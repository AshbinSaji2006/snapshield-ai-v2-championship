# SnapShield AI v2 — 90-second pitch

“Suspicious messages and screenshots can contain banking details, identity data, and authentication instructions. Cloud-dependent security analysis may add a remote-processing boundary. SnapShield AI keeps its core analysis local: your data never needs to leave your device to protect your device.

It is more than a spam classifier. A message, URL, screenshot, or QR code is examined by Fast and Deep ONNX guards, URL forensics, OCR, QR decoding, PII and brand/domain analysis, and deterministic security rules. An adaptive router reserves Deep Guard for ambiguous or contextual cases. A Threat Evidence Graph shows how signals connect before bounded risk fusion returns a reason and a safe action.

On the untouched 766-message UCI test, Deep Guard reached 94.44 percent positive recall. On an independently sourced, strictly UCI-deduplicated 521-message 2022 Mendeley holdout, it reached 98.04 percent recall. Adaptive routing preserved UCI recall with a 0.30 percent false-positive rate. These are dataset-specific SMS results, not universal phishing accuracy.

The working app verifies local ONNX inference with CPUExecutionProvider. Its QNN path, QDQ model candidate, exact-artifact gate, and AI Hub automation are implemented; Snapdragon NPU hardware validation is pending, so we claim no NPU performance yet.

SnapShield AI is private AI, local security, designed for Snapdragon.”
